Support Halalkartet

Halalkartet is free to use, and no place can pay for a better status. It will stay that way. That is why the map is run by volunteers and by gifts from people like you.

About us

Halalkartet is a small project made to make it easier to find halal food in Oslo. The idea is simple. If you are out with friends and get hungry, or want to try somewhere new, the map shows you where you can eat and how certain the halal status is.

The colours on the map tell you how safe the food is.

Green means the place is confirmed one hundred percent halal.
Orange means parts of the menu are not halal.
Grey means we have not yet confirmed the halal status. It does not mean the place is not halal, only that you should check for yourself.

The pins also have symbols: حلال, an exclamation mark and a question mark. That way you can tell them apart even if you cannot tell the colours apart.

We are starting in Oslo and will add more places over time, and eventually more cities. If you have a tip or a correction, we really appreciate you letting us know.

Privacy

Halalkartet is made to respect your privacy. Here we briefly explain what happens to data when you use the site.

No account and no tracking

You do not need an account to use Halalkartet. We use no tracking cookies and show no ads.

Visitor statistics

We count how many people visit the site with Google Analytics, set up without cookies and with an anonymised IP address. The count is anonymous and aggregated, and we do not build a profile of you.

Location

If you tap Near me, the browser asks for your location to show places nearby. The location is only used in your browser there and then. It is not stored, and it is not sent to us.

Directions

If you tap Directions, Google Maps opens in a new tab with the address of the place. Google’s own terms and privacy rules then apply, not ours.

The forms

If you send us a tip or a message, we receive what you write: your name if you give it, where you are from, phone or email, and the message itself. We only use it to reply to you and to improve the map, and we do not share it with anyone. If you want us to delete something you have sent, let us know and we will.

The forms are sent through Formspree, which forwards them to us by email. Formspree keeps submissions for a limited time so they arrive, and sees technical details such as IP address to stop spam.

Map and hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The map tiles are loaded from Stadia Maps, which builds them from OpenStreetMap and OpenMapTiles data. These services may see technical details such as IP address in order to deliver the map and keep the site running.

Ofte stilte spørsmål

Hva betyr fargene på kartet?

Grønn betyr bekreftet hundre prosent halal. Oransje betyr at deler av menyen ikke er halal. Grå betyr at vi ikke har bekreftet halalstatusen ennå. Det betyr ikke at stedet ikke er halal, bare at vi ikke vet. Tegnene i pinnene skiller nivåene også: حلال, utropstegn og spørsmålstegn.

Hvordan vet dere at et sted er halal?

Vi sjekker hvert sted så godt vi kan, for eksempel om det henger et synlig sertifikat, hva personalet sier, eller om stedet er godt kjent for å være halal. Er vi usikre, merker vi stedet grått.

Halalkartet sertifiserer ikke selv. Vi viser kilden, datoen og hvem som sa det, så du kan vurdere selv. Står det ingen dato på et sted, har vi ikke bekreftet det ennå, og det står i kortet.

Hva betyr dollartegnene?

De viser omtrentlig prisnivå. $ er rimelig, $$ er middels, og $$$ er dyrere. Du kan filtrere på prisnivå.

Hvor pålitelige er åpningstidene?

Klokkeslettene følger norsk tid uansett hvor du selv befinner deg. Grønt betyr åpent, gult at det stenger om under en time, og rødt at det er stengt.

Vær oppmerksom på at vi foreløpig bare har registrert når stedene stenger, ikke når de åpner. Tidlig på dagen kan et sted derfor vises som åpent selv om det ikke har åpnet ennå. Ring gjerne først hvis du er ute i god tid.

Hva er «Utvalgte steder»?

Fem steder fra kartet som vises fram om gangen, og som byttes ut hver femte dag. Utvalget er det samme for alle og går etter tur gjennom hele lista.

Kan jeg dele et sted med noen?

Ja. Åpne stedet på kartet og trykk Del, så får du en lenke som åpner kartet rett på det stedet.

Kan jeg foreslå et sted eller melde fra om en feil?

Ja, og det setter vi stor pris på. Trykk Tips oss nede til venstre på kartet, eller bruk skjemaet på kontaktsiden.

Hvilke steder kommer med på kartet?

Serverer stedet både halal mat og svin, kommer det med som delvis halal og merkes oransje, med en linje i kortet om hva som ikke er halal. Da kan du avgjøre selv om det er greit for deg. Steder uten noe halal på menyen kommer ikke med.

Serverer de alkohol å drikke, men ikke bruker det i maten, kan stedet merkes halal. Da står det en tydelig merknad i kortet om at de serverer halal mat, men også alkoholholdig drikke, så du kan bestemme selv.

Det samme gjelder når bare deler av menyen er halal av andre grunner: stedet merkes oransje, og kortet sier hva som gjelder.

Dekker dere hele Norge?

Vi begynner i Oslo og utvider til flere byer etter hvert.

Koster det noe?

Nei, Halalkartet er helt gratis å bruke.

Frequently asked questions

What do the colours on the map mean?

Green means confirmed one hundred percent halal. Orange means parts of the menu are not halal. Grey means we have not confirmed the halal status yet. It does not mean the place is not halal, only that we do not know. The symbols in the pins also tell the levels apart: حلال, an exclamation mark and a question mark.

How do you know a place is halal?

We check each place as well as we can, for example whether a certificate is visible, what the staff say, or whether the place is well known for being halal. If we are unsure, we mark the place grey.

Halalkartet does not certify places itself. We show the source, the date and who said it, so you can judge for yourself. If a place has no date, we have not confirmed it yet, and the card says so.

What do the dollar signs mean?

They show the approximate price level. $ is inexpensive, $$ is moderate, and $$$ is pricier. You can filter by price.

How reliable are the opening hours?

The times follow Norwegian time wherever you are. Green means open, yellow that it closes within an hour, and red that it is closed.

Please note that so far we have only recorded when places close, not when they open. Early in the day a place may therefore show as open even if it has not opened yet. Feel free to call first if you are out early.

What are “Featured places”?

Five places from the map shown at a time, changed every fifth day. The selection is the same for everyone and goes through the whole list in turn.

Can I share a place with someone?

Yes. Open the place on the map and tap Share, and you get a link that opens the map right on that place.

Can I suggest a place or report an error?

Yes, and we really appreciate it. Tap Suggest a place on the map, or use the form on the contact page.

Which places are included on the map?

If a place serves both halal food and pork, it is included as partly halal and marked orange, with a line in the card about what is not halal. Then you can decide for yourself whether it is fine for you. Places with nothing halal on the menu are not included.

If they serve alcohol to drink but do not use it in the food, the place can be marked halal. The card then has a clear note that they serve halal food but also alcoholic drinks, so you can decide for yourself.

The same applies when only parts of the menu are halal for other reasons: the place is marked orange, and the card says what applies.

Do you cover all of Norway?

We are starting in Oslo and will expand to more cities over time.

Does it cost anything?

No, Halalkartet is completely free to use.
